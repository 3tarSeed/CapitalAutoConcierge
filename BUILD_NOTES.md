# Capital Auto Concierge — private review

Working brand, not approved launch name. Human-led sales: estimate → request → call → inspection → final quote → manually coordinated fulfillment.

Includes vehicle capture, seven service groups, sourced preliminary benchmarks, flexible/deadline/urgent timing, fulfillment preferences, consent and durable lead storage. Private authenticated owner-scoped dashboard supports status, notes, assignment and follow-up date. No automatic messages, appointments, payment, rush pricing or subcontractor assignment.

Before public launch: approve brand, rate card and geography; choose production hosting/database (original discussion considered Netlify/Supabase); implement staff allowlist and public lead ingestion with anti-abuse protection; approve privacy notice, retention, terms and communication process. Current API requires private authenticated identity and must not be treated as public customer intake. Photos are requested manually after contact in this slice. No guarantee of mobile eligibility or transport availability.

Pricing citations and qualifications are available at /pricing. Raw model/trim entry intentionally avoids suggesting a complete vehicle database. Price calculation is server-side, snapshots versioned, idempotent request IDs, prepared SQL, server-side owner scoping, origin checks. No contact data stored in browser persistence.
